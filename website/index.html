About MPIS

Learning that blends academics, character, and creativity.

Maryland Prestigious International School is a vibrant community in Lagos where students are inspired to question, collaborate, and lead. Our dedicated teachers personalize learning, while our pastoral team ensures every child is seen, safe, and celebrated.

Small class advantage

Teacher-to-student ratio that amplifies voice and agency.

Proven outcomes

Graduates excel in top universities across Africa, Europe, and North America.

Beyond the classroom

Creative arts, leadership labs, & service learning.

Academic Pathways

Rigorous programs tailored to every learner.

From inquiry-based early years to Cambridge-aligned upper school, students grow through challenge, mentorship, and real-world problem-solving.

View entry requirements

Early Years

Playful learning with literacy corners, numeracy games, sensory labs, and social-emotional coaching for ages 3-6.

Primary & Lower Secondary

Interdisciplinary projects, outdoor science, coding, and public speaking develop curiosity and confidence.

College Prep

Cambridge IGCSE and WAEC pathways with personalized counseling, internships, and leadership internships.

Campus Life

A dynamic, joyful campus experience.

Our campus brings together creative arts studios, a digital media lab, science innovation center, sports complex, and serene boarding houses with dedicated dorm parents.

Innovation Studio

Robotics league, 3D printing, and coding clubs guided by Google CS First certified coaches.

Creative Arts

Visual arts, music production, drama productions, and an annual cultural festival that celebrates our community.

Sports & Wellness

Athletics, swimming, basketball, soccer, and yoga with certified coaches and a modern fitness center.
